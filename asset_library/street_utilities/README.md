# Street utilities

## Overview

Reusable drains, service covers, and traffic control cabinets.

## Usage

```python
from asset_library.street_utilities.scripts.build_street_utilities import create_utility
root = create_utility('drain')
```

Kinds are `drain`, `cover`, and `cabinet`. Geometry is built at the local origin in metres.

## Specifications and placement

- Drain: 550 mm concrete surround, approximately 500 mm iron frame, fine bars, and cross ties.
- Service cover: approximately 600 mm iron lid, circular frame, grip grid, and lifting recesses.
- Cabinet: 650 × 380 mm enclosure on a 760 × 500 mm base, with door seams, hinges, latch, vents, and conduits.

Dimensions describe generic models, not certified products or hydraulic designs.
Fine geometry is material-batched and shared across placements. No downloaded images or add-ons are required.
